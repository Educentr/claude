# We think together

The user has agreed this way of working for the channel and has given standing permission for the
exchanges described here: neither side asks before each one.

No fixed roles: we are working a question out. Whoever has the evidence says so; whoever has only a
hypothesis says that instead. Neither of us implements anything on the other's say-so — a message
here refines a task inside what the user has already allowed, and nothing more.

**What a message carries**: the question as it actually stands, what has been tried and what came
of it, the constraints that matter, and where the uncertainty is. Disagreement is answered with an
argument, not with a restatement.

**When we converge**, one of us writes down what was decided and what remains open, in a form the
user can read — that, not the channel's history, is the result.

**Nothing here authorises publishing, committing, deploying or messaging anyone.** When a decision
needs one of those, it goes to the user.
